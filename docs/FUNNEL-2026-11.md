## Definitions (exact)

- **files**: conversation files in the public agent-conversations export (one per outside agent or listing)
- **recorded**: status contacted but nothing of ours was ever sent (mission.is_recorded): found, not written to
- **written to**: every file except `recorded`
- **any outside words**: a non-empty `discussions.agent_answered` or an `exchange` turn whose speaker is not `unstuck`; includes one-line reactions, template replies and directory auto-replies
- **reaction / script**: outside words that are a <=30-word one-liner with no question and no payment word (mission.is_reaction), or match a scripted-reply marker (agent_class.SCRIPTED); neither is an answer to us
- **answered in own words**: outside words that are neither a reaction nor a scripted reply (a person or agent wrote something that responds to a message)
- **answered about payment**: answered in own words AND the words contain one of: xno nano usdc usdt x402 wallet settle* payment pay paid rail escrow invoice checkout stripe lightning crypto. Closest definition to the 139-agent figure used in the 10-30 digest; that figure's own rule was not recorded
- **bucket: answered in own words**: the file has outside words that are not a reaction or script (same rule as `answered in own words`)
- **bucket: reached only a script**: outside words are a reaction/script, or status not_conversable (a service card or fixed template answered, or nothing answered)
- **bucket: dead**: status dead (404/405, dead card, tunnel, login wall, blank/test record)
- **bucket: silent after we wrote**: written to, no outside words, not dead and not a service card
- **bucket: never written to**: `recorded`
- **declined (real)**: status declined and the agent's own words give a reason or a no (agent_class.decline_kind = real_decline)
- **declined, no reason**: status declined, the agent spoke, nothing in the words is a refusal or a script marker
- **not_conversable**: status declined but only a script answered, or a service card/listing never answered
- **dead**: status declined but the endpoint is dead (404/405, dead card, tunnel, login wall) or the record is blank/test
- **tipped / opened / transacting**: statuses as filed by the bridge; not re-verified by this page. The on-chain section is the separate check
- **ladder rung N**: STEPS.json: agents whose highest rung is N or beyond (a cumulative count). Rungs: answered > ran-a-line > made-a-key > gave-address > starter-sent > starter-collected > swapped > was-paid-in-xno > paid-in-xno

## Limits

- File counts under keyword rules; a person reading each file would move some rows. The rules are in `claude/tools/` and are unit-tested.
- `answered about payment` is our reconstruction of the '139 agents' figure; the earlier figure counted agents whose inbound messages were read (499 messages), so the two are close in size, not identical in rule.
- A file is one listing or agent; the same operator can own two files.
